Culture Benefits

How we work, and what we offer

Williams and Rocker is a team of 250 engineers, designers and analysts building software for clients across finance, retail and healthcare. This page describes our culture and the benefits every employee receives.

Company culture

We hire thoughtful people, give them clear goals and trust them to do their best work. We prefer honest conversations over politics, and written decisions over hallway agreements.

What we value

Own the outcome

Teams decide how to solve a problem and stay accountable for the result, from first idea to production support.

Learn in the open

We share what worked and what did not in writing, through blog posts, demos and post-project reviews, so others can build on it.

Respect people's time

Meetings have an agenda and an owner. Decisions are written down so nobody has to attend to stay informed.

Be kind and direct

We give feedback early, specifically and with care. We assume good intent and ask questions before drawing conclusions.

How we work

Flexible hours

Each team agrees on four hours of core overlap. The rest of the day is yours to arrange around your life.

Hybrid by default

Offices in Mumbai and Bengaluru are open for collaboration days. Remote work is supported for most roles.

Regular feedback

Monthly one-to-one check-ins with your manager, plus a formal review twice a year focused on growth rather than ratings.

Diverse and inclusive teams

We recruit from varied backgrounds, run structured interviews and maintain employee groups that shape our policies.

Community

Volunteer days

Two paid days each year to volunteer with a cause of your choice.

Hack weeks

Twice a year, everyone can spend a week on a side idea, a tool or a skill they want to try.

Benefits

Our benefits are designed to support your health, your time and your growth. They apply to all full-time employees from the first day of work.

Health and wellbeing

Medical insurance

Group health cover for you, your spouse and up to two dependent children, including hospitalisation and maternity.

Wellness allowance

An annual allowance for gym membership, sports, yoga or counselling sessions.

Mental health support

Free, confidential counselling sessions for employees and their families through our employee assistance programme.

Annual health check

A full preventive health check-up every year, paid for by the company.

Time off

Paid annual leave

24 days of paid leave each year, plus public holidays.

Sick leave

12 days of paid sick leave each year, with no documentation needed for short absences.

Parental leave

26 weeks of paid leave for birth parents and 12 weeks for all other new parents, including adoption.

Year-end shutdown

The office closes between Christmas and New Year, and this time does not count against your leave.

Financial

Provident fund

Company contribution to your provident fund in line with statutory rules, with an optional higher contribution.

Performance bonus

An annual bonus linked to company and team results.

Referral rewards

A cash reward when someone you refer joins and completes six months with us.

Life and accident cover

Group term life and personal accident insurance at no cost to you.

Growth and learning

Learning budget

An annual budget for courses, books, conferences and certifications of your choice.

Mentorship

Every new joiner is paired with a mentor for their first year, and anyone can request one later.

Internal mobility

After 12 months in a role you can apply to move to another team without needing your manager's approval.

Everyday perks

Home office setup

A one-time allowance for a desk, chair and monitor, plus a monthly internet reimbursement.

Meals and snacks

Free lunch and snacks on office days, with vegetarian, vegan and allergy-friendly options.

Commute support

Shuttle service from major transit points, or a monthly travel allowance.
